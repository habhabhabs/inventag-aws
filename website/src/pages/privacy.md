# Privacy and cookies

This site keeps things to the essentials. Nothing optional runs unless you
say yes.

## Essential: always on

These are stored in your browser's local storage, on your device only. They
are not sent anywhere.

| Name | What it remembers | How long |
|---|---|---|
| `inventag-analytics` | Your cookie choice: `granted` or `denied` | Until you clear your browser data |
| `theme` | Light, dark or system appearance, if you picked one | Until you clear your browser data |

The fonts are hosted on this site, so reading a page makes no requests to
font services.

## Analytics (only if you allow it)

If you choose "Allow analytics", the site loads Google Analytics 4. It
collects page-visit statistics: which pages are viewed, roughly where
visitors come from, and the device and browser type. Visits are tied to a
random ID stored in a cookie, not to your name. It helps the maintainers see
which pages people find useful. Advertising features stay off: the site
declines consent for ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation.

| Cookie | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Tells one visitor's page views apart from another's | Up to 2 years |
| `_ga_<id>` | Keeps the current session together | Up to 2 years |

How the provider handles this data is described in its
[privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in
[how it uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

If you choose "Just the essentials", close the banner, or simply ignore it,
no analytics code is loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

## Changing your mind

Use "Cookie preferences" at the bottom of any page. If you switch analytics
off, the site stops sending page visits straight away and deletes the `_ga`
cookies. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information,
such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service. See the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
